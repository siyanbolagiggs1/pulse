The problem

Influencer marketing is expensive, and the money pools in a few hands.

For businesses

Real social reach costs real money. Influencer marketing spend is projected at $32 to $34 billion globally in 2026, growing over 30% a year. Small businesses are priced out entirely.

For everyday people

Millions of ordinary social media users have real audiences, just not large enough to be "influencers." There is no way for them to get paid for something as simple as reposting content they'd share anyway.

The solution

A paid repost marketplace, priced by trust, not follower count.

This model already works, proven at scale in web3, where projects pay ordinary users to repost and engage instead of hiring expensive influencers. Pulse brings that same playbook to real businesses, with real bank payouts instead of tokens.

01

Fund a campaign

A business posts a repost campaign with a budget, a payout rate, and eligibility rules, funded from their wallet.

02

Repost and submit proof

Any user reposts the content and submits a link plus screenshot. No follower minimums required.

03

Get paid, fairly

Payout is priced by an influence score, not just follower count, and paid out via real Paystack bank transfer.

Influence scoring

Every payout is priced by a 0 to 100 score built from followers, engagement, account age, and track record, not celebrity status. Better, more credible accounts earn more per repost.

Fraud detection

Hard blocks on fresh accounts, duplicate submissions, and abnormal engagement, plus a trust score that auto-suspends bad actors. Verification is the product, not an afterthought.

A large, fast growing market, with the payment rails finally in place.

That entire spend concentrates in a small pool of high follower accounts. Nigeria, South Africa, and Kenya are Africa's top three influencer markets, and Pulse already supports Paystack payouts across all of them, plus Ghana, without any rework.

Business model

A 20% commission on every payout. We only earn when value is delivered.

Usage based revenue

Pulse takes a 20% platform commission the moment a repost submission is approved and paid out. Revenue scales directly with campaign spend, not subscriptions or signups.

Worked example

On a ₦10,000 campaign, Pulse earns ₦2,000 in gross commission. After Paystack's processing and payout fees (about ₦275), net revenue lands around ₦1,725, a real take rate near 17%.
